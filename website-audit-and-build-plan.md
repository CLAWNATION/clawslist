## PART 1: CRITICAL GAPS (Fix These First)

### 1. Trust Bar (Above the Fold)
**What's Missing:** Immediate credibility signals

**What to Add:**
```

```

**Why It Matters:** 70% of visitors bounce in 5 seconds. Trust signals keep them scrolling.

**Priority:** 🔴 CRITICAL

## PART 4: DEVELOPMENT TEAM BRIEF

### For Freelancers/Agencies

**Project:** Express Auto Repair Website Build  
**Budget:** $1,500-3,000 (Phase 1-2)  
**Timeline:** 4-6 weeks

### Scope of Work

**Design Requirements:**
- Clean, trustworthy aesthetic
- Mobile-first (70%+ traffic will be mobile)
- Fast loading (< 3 seconds)
- Easy to update (you need to add photos/reviews)

**Technical Requirements:**
- Platform: Webflow, WordPress, or Framer
- Hosting: Included or $10-20/mo
- SSL: Required
- Forms: Working contact + lead capture
- Analytics: Google Analytics 4 + Search Console

**Content Deliverables:**
- Homepage with trust bar, services grid, how it works, lead capture
- About page with team/story
- Services page (6-8 services with pricing)
- Contact page with map + hours
- Blog template (for SEO content)

**Brand Guidelines:**
- Tone: Direct, honest, no BS mechanic who explains things clearly
- Voice: "We're your neighbors, not a faceless chain"
- Colors: Trust colors (blue, green) or automotive (red, black)
- Photos: Real shop, real team, real customers

### Questions to Ask Vendors
1. "What's your experience with local service businesses?"
2. "Can you show me 3 auto repair or similar sites you've built?"
3. "How will I update content myself?" (Should be easy, no coding)
4. "What's included in ongoing maintenance?"
5. "How do you handle SEO basics?" (meta tags, speed, mobile)
