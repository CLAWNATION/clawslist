## PHASE 3: ONGOING OPTIMIZATION (Weekly)

### Google Posts (2-3x per week)
Posts boost visibility and engagement. Types:

**"What's New" Posts:**
- Shop updates, new equipment, team additions
- Example: "Just added new diagnostic scanner — faster, more accurate checks!"

**"Offers" Posts:**
- Limited-time promotions
- Example: "$XX off brake service this month. Book now!"

**"Products" Posts:**
- Highlight specific services
- Example: "Pre-purchase inspections — don't buy a lemon!"

**"Events" Posts:**
- Community involvement, car care clinics

**Post formula:**
- Eye-catching photo
- Headline (5-10 words)
- 2-3 sentences of copy
- CTA button (Book, Call, Learn more)
- Link to website when relevant

### Google Q&A (Seed immediately)
Add common questions and answers yourself:

**Q: Do I need an appointment?**
A: Nope! Walk-ins are welcome for oil changes and most services. For bigger jobs, calling ahead helps us order parts and give you faster turnaround.

**Q: How much does a diagnostic cost?**
A: Free! We'll scan your car, identify the issue, and give you a straight quote. No charge for the diagnosis.

**Q: Do you offer a warranty?**
A: Yes — 24 months/24,000 miles on parts and labor. If something goes wrong, bring it back and we'll make it right.

**Q: Can you work on [make/model]?**
A: We work on all makes and models — foreign and domestic. From Honda Civics to Ford F-150s, we've got you covered.

**Q: Do you offer financing?**
A: Yes! Ask us about payment plans for larger repairs.

**Pro tip:** Monitor Q&A daily and respond to new questions within 2 hours.

### Reviews Management (Daily)

**Review request strategy:**
1. **Ask happy customers in person:** "Would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It really helps local businesses like ours."
2. **Follow up via text/email:** Send link 24 hours after service
3. **Make it easy:** Create short link: `g.page/expressauto/review`

**Review response templates:**

**5-star response:**
> "Thanks [Name]! 🙌 We're glad we could get you back on the road quickly. Thanks for trusting us with your [car model]. See you next time!"

**4-star response:**
> "Thanks for the review, [Name]! We appreciate your feedback. If there's anything we can do better next time, please let us know. We're always looking to improve!"

**3-star or below:**
> "[Name], I'm [Owner Name], the owner. This isn't the experience we want anyone to have. Can you give me a call at (XXX) XXX-XXXX? I'd like to understand what happened and make this right."

**Response rules:**
- [ ] Respond to ALL reviews within 24 hours
- [ ] Use customer's name
- [ ] Mention specific service when possible
- [ ] Invite back for future service
- [ ] Take negative reviews offline (ask them to call)

**Review goals:**
- Month 1: 10 reviews
- Month 3: 50 reviews
- Month 6: 100 reviews
- Ongoing: 5-10 new reviews per month

## TROUBLESHOOTING

**Profile suspended?**
- Don't create a new one — contact Google support
- Common causes: keyword stuffing in name, fake reviews, wrong category

**Not ranking?**
- Check: Is address hidden? (Should be visible for auto repair)
- Check: Are you in the right category?
- Check: Do you have enough reviews? (Need 20+ to compete)
- Check: Is your website optimized for local SEO?

**Duplicate listing?**
- Report to Google — don't try to manage both
- Merge if possible

**Negative review attack?**
- Respond professionally to all
- Flag fake reviews for removal
- Drown out with positive review requests to happy customers
