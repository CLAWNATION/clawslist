## PHASE 1: FOUNDATION (Week 1)

### 1. Create Review Request Templates

#### Template A: In-Person Ask (Most Effective)

**Timing:** Right after service completion, customer is satisfied

**Script:**
```
"Hey [Name], I'm glad we could get your [car/issue] sorted out today. 

Quick question: Would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It 
really helps local businesses like ours show up when people search 
for mechanics in [City].

It takes 30 seconds — just search 'Express Auto Repair' on Google 
and click 'Write a review.'"
```

**Follow-up:**
```
"I'll text you the link right now so you have it handy. Thanks so much — 
we really appreciate it!"
```

#### Template B: SMS Follow-Up (Same Day)

**Timing:** 2-4 hours after service

**Message:**
```
Hi [Name]! This is [Your Name] from Express Auto Repair. Thanks again 
for trusting us with your [car model] today.

If you have 30 seconds, would you mind leaving us a quick review? 
It really helps other [City] drivers find honest mechanics.

[Google Review Link]

No pressure at all — just grateful for your business! 🙏
```

#### Template C: Email Follow-Up (Next Day)

**Timing:** 24 hours after service

**Subject:** How's your [car] running? + A quick favor

**Body:**
```
Hi [Name],

Just checking in — how's your [car model] running after yesterday's 
[service]? Hopefully everything is smooth and quiet!

If you're happy with the work, would you mind leaving us a quick 
Google review? It takes less than a minute and helps other [City] 
drivers find a mechanic they can trust.

[LEAVE A REVIEW BUTTON]

No pressure at all — just wanted to follow up and say thanks for 
choosing Express Auto Repair.

If anything doesn't feel right with the car, just reply to this email 
or give me a call at [phone]. We stand behind our work 100%.

Thanks again,
[Your Name]
Express Auto Repair
[Phone] | [Website]
```

### 2. Create QR Code for In-Shop

**Tool:** [qr-code-generator.com](https://www.qr-code-generator.com) or Canva

**Destination:** Direct link to Google review form
```
https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=[YOUR_PLACE_ID]
```

**To find your Place ID:**
1. Go to [Google Place ID Finder](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/javascript/examples/places-placeid-finder)
2. Search your business
3. Copy the Place ID
4. Use URL format above

**QR Code Placement:**
- [ ] Reception desk (small stand)
- [ ] Payment counter (sticker)
- [ ] Customer waiting area (poster)
- [ ] Key drop box
- [ ] Business cards (back side)
- [ ] Invoice/receipt footer

### 3. Set Up Automated Review Requests

**Option A: Through Your POS/Shop Management System**
If using:
- Shopmonkey
- AutoFluent
- Mitchell1

Configure automatic review request 24 hours after service completion.

**Option B: Manual System (Free)**
Use Google Calendar + Phone Reminders:
1. End of each day, add next-day follow-ups to calendar
2. Set SMS reminder for 2 PM (2 hours after typical completion)
3. Set email reminder for next day

**Option C: Mailchimp Integration**
- Add customers to "Recent Service" segment
- Trigger automated email 24 hours after service
- Use template from email-capture-form-copy.md

## PHASE 2: SYSTEMATIC EXECUTION (Weeks 2-12)

### The Review Request Workflow

Every customer goes through this funnel:

```
Service Completion
    ↓
In-Person Ask (100% of customers)
    ↓
SMS Follow-Up (if not reviewed within 4 hours)
    ↓
Email Follow-Up (if not reviewed within 24 hours)
    ↓
Second Ask (if loyal customer, 1 week later)
```

### Daily Routine (10 minutes)

**End of Day:**
- [ ] Export today's customers from POS/records
- [ ] Add to follow-up list
- [ ] Schedule SMS for tomorrow 2 PM
- [ ] Schedule email for tomorrow 10 AM

**Next Morning:**
- [ ] Check yesterday's reviews
- [ ] Respond to all new reviews
- [ ] Check follow-up list for non-responders

### Review Response Templates

**5-Star Review Response:**
```
Thanks [Name]! 🙌 We're glad we could get your [car/issue] fixed quickly. 
See you next time!
```

**4-Star Review Response:**
```
Thanks for the review, [Name]! We appreciate your feedback. If there's 
anything we can do better next time, please let us know. We're always 
looking to improve!
```

**3-Star or Below Response:**
```
[Name], I'm [Owner Name], the owner. This isn't the experience we want 
anyone to have. Can you give me a call at [phone]? I'd like to understand 
what happened and make this right.
```

**Response Rules:**
- Respond within 24 hours
- Use customer's name
- Mention specific service if possible
- Invite back for future service
- Take negative reviews offline immediately

## INCENTIVES (ETHICAL & EFFECTIVE)

### What's Allowed by Google

✅ **OK:**
- Asking satisfied customers
- Making it easy (QR codes, direct links)
- Thanking reviewers
- Small thank-you gifts ($10 off next visit)

❌ **NOT OK:**
- Paying for reviews
- Offering discounts FOR reviews
- Review gating (only asking happy customers)
- Asking employees/family to review
- Review swapping with other businesses

### Ethical Incentive Ideas

1. **"Review & Save"**
   - "$10 off your next visit as a thank you for leaving feedback"
   - Give to everyone, regardless of rating
   - Focus on feedback, not just 5 stars

2. **Monthly Drawing**
   - Every reviewer entered to win free oil change
   - One winner per month
   - Announced on social media

3. **Referral + Review Combo**
   - "Leave a review and get a referral card"
   - Referral card: "$25 off for you, $25 off for friend"

## TROUBLESHOOTING

### Low Conversion Rate (<15%)

**Possible causes:**
- Asking too late (customer already left)
- Not asking in person
- Complicated process

**Fixes:**
- Ask immediately after payment
- Use QR codes (instant)
- Train all staff to ask
- Follow up same day

### Getting 1-3 Star Reviews

**Don't panic.** This is actually good data.

**Response:**
1. Reply publicly within 1 hour
2. Acknowledge the issue
3. Take it offline: "Please call me directly"
4. Fix the problem
5. Ask for update if resolved

**Prevention:**
- Review requests same day (catch issues early)
- "How was everything?" before they leave
- Fix problems before they become reviews

### Reviews Not Showing Up

**Common reasons:**
- Customer doesn't have Google account
- Review flagged as spam
- Customer used fake name
- Multiple reviews from same IP

**Fix:**
- Ask customers to use real name
- Don't submit reviews yourself
- Space out review requests

### Fake Negative Reviews

**From competitors or trolls:**

1. **Flag it:** Click "Flag as inappropriate" in Google
2. **Respond professionally:** "We have no record of this customer..."
3. **Report to Google:** Use GBP support
4. **Drown it out:** More positive reviews push it down
