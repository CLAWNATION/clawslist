## DESIGNER NOTES

### Do:
- Keep it scannable (lots of white space)
- Use icons sparingly but consistently
- Ensure checkboxes are clearly printable
- Maintain consistent spacing
- Test print at actual size

### Don't:
- Crowd pages with too much text
- Use more than 3 font sizes per page
- Forget about people who print in B&W
- Make red flags actually red (use burgundy/dark red for print)

### Accessibility:
- Ensure 4.5:1 contrast ratio for text
- Checkboxes large enough to hand-write in
- Clear hierarchy for screen readers (if digital)
