# The Honest Mechanic's Used Car Checklist

**A 15-Point Inspection Guide Before You Buy**

---

## INTRODUCTION

Buying a used car? Don't get stuck with someone else's problem. This checklist is the same one our mechanics use when evaluating pre-purchase inspections. It's saved our customers thousands in surprise repair bills.

**How to use this:**
- Print it out and bring it to the test drive
- Check off each item as you inspect
- If 3+ items are questionable, walk away or negotiate hard

---

## THE 15-POINT CHECKLIST

### 1. COLD START BEHAVIOR
**What to do:** Let the car sit overnight, then start it first thing in the morning.

**Red flags:**
- ☐ Hard starting (cranks more than 3 seconds)
- ☐ Rough idle that doesn't smooth out in 30 seconds
- ☐ Knocking or ticking sounds
- ☐ Blue smoke from exhaust (burning oil)

**Why it matters:** Cold-start issues often mean expensive engine or fuel system problems.

---

### 2. CHECK ENGINE LIGHT
**What to do:** Look at the dashboard when you start the car.

**Red flags:**
- ☐ Light stays on after starting
- ☐ Light was "just reset" (ask for history)
- ☐ Seller refuses OBD scan

**Why it matters:** A check engine light could be a $50 sensor or a $3,000 catalytic converter.

**Pro tip:** Bring a $20 OBD scanner or ask AutoZone to scan it for free.

---

### 3. TRANSMISSION SHIFTING
**What to do:** Drive at various speeds. Feel for smooth acceleration.

**Red flags:**
- ☐ Hard or jerky shifts
- ☐ Delayed engagement (gas pedal to movement lag)
- ☐ Slipping RPMs without acceleration
- ☐ Strange noises in reverse

**Why it matters:** Transmission rebuilds cost $2,000-4,000. Don't skip this.

---

### 4. BRAKE FEEL AND RESPONSE
**What to do:** Test brakes at low speed in a safe area.

**Red flags:**
- ☐ Spongy or soft brake pedal
- ☐ Pulling to one side when braking
n- ☐ Grinding or squealing noises
- ☐ Vibration in steering wheel when braking

**Why it matters:** Brakes are safety-critical. Issues here are non-negotiable repairs.

---

### 5. SUSPENSION AND STEERING
**What to do:** Drive over bumps. Turn the wheel fully left and right.

**Red flags:**
- ☐ Clunking or knocking over bumps
- ☐ Steering wheel shakes at highway speeds
- ☐ Car drifts or requires constant correction
- ☐ Excessive body roll in turns

**Why it matters:** Suspension repairs add up fast ($500-1,500 per axle).

---

### 6. TIRE CONDITION
**What to do:** Inspect all four tires. Check the spare.

**Red flags:**
- ☐ Less than 4/32" tread depth (use quarter test)
- ☐ Uneven wear patterns (indicates alignment issues)
- ☐ Sidewall cracks or bulges
- ☐ Mismatched tire brands/sizes
- ☐ No spare or damaged spare

**Why it matters:** New tires cost $400-800. Uneven wear signals alignment/suspension problems.

---

### 7. FLUID CHECKS
**What to do:** Check all fluids with engine cool. Look under car for leaks.

| Fluid | Good Sign | Bad Sign |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| Engine oil | Amber/clean, proper level | Black, low, milky, or metallic bits |
| Coolant | Bright color, proper level | Brown, low, or oily residue |
| Brake fluid | Clear/golden | Dark, low, or dirty |
| Transmission | Red/pink (auto), proper level | Brown/black, burnt smell |
| Power steering | Clear/amber | Low, dark, or foamy |

**Why it matters:** Fluid condition tells you how well the car was maintained.

---

### 8. BODY AND FRAME
**What to do:** Walk around the car. Look closely at panel gaps and paint.

**Red flags:**
- ☐ Misaligned panels or uneven gaps
- ☐ Paint overspray on trim/rubber
- ☐ Rust bubbles (especially rocker panels, wheel wells)
- ☐ Welding marks in trunk or engine bay
- ☐ Doors/hood/trunk that don't close smoothly

**Why it matters:** Frame damage or poor repairs mean the car was in a serious accident.

---

### 9. INTERIOR CONDITION
**What to do:** Sit in every seat. Test all electronics.

**Red flags:**
- ☐ Airbag warning light
- ☐ Non-working windows, locks, or mirrors
- ☐ A/C that doesn't blow cold (expensive fix)
- ☐ Check engine or warning lights
- ☐ Musty smell (water damage/mold)
- ☐ Excessive wear for claimed mileage

**Why it matters:** Interior issues are annoying, but also indicators of neglect or flood damage.

---

### 10. EXHAUST SYSTEM
**What to do:** Start the car. Listen at the tailpipe.

**Red flags:**
- ☐ Loud rumbling (hole in exhaust)
- ☐ Hissing sounds (leak)
- ☐ Rotten egg smell (catalytic converter issue)
- ☐ Visible rust holes in muffler/pipes

**Why it matters:** Exhaust repairs range from $200-1,000+. Failed emissions = no registration.

---

### 11. ELECTRICAL SYSTEM
**What to do:** Test everything electrical.

**Red flags:**
- ☐ Weak or slow cranking
- ☐ Dimming headlights at idle
- ☐ Non-working power accessories
- ☐ Flickering dashboard lights
- ☐ Battery more than 3 years old

**Why it matters:** Electrical gremlins are hard to diagnose and expensive to fix.

---

### 12. UNDER THE CAR
**What to do:** Get on the ground. Look underneath.

**Red flags:**
- ☐ Active fluid leaks (oil, coolant, transmission)
- ☐ Fresh undercoating (hiding rust)
- ☐ Bent or damaged frame components
- ☐ Missing heat shields
- ☐ Excessive rust on floor pans

**Why it matters:** Undercarriage issues are often hidden and expensive.

---

### 13. MAINTENANCE RECORDS
**What to ask for:** Service history, receipts, CarFax/AutoCheck.

**Red flags:**
- ☐ No records available
- ☐ Long gaps in maintenance
- ☐ Recent "flip" (bought at auction 30 days ago)
- ☐ Multiple owners in short time
- ☐ Salvage/rebuilt title

**Why it matters:** Well-maintained cars last longer. Records prove care.

---

### 14. TEST DRIVE CHECKLIST
**What to do:** Drive for at least 15 minutes. Include highway and stop-and-go.

**Check for:**
- ☐ Smooth acceleration through all gears
- ☐ No vibrations at any speed
- ☐ Quiet cabin (no wind/road noise from bad seals)
- ☐ All gauges reading normally
- ☐ Cruise control works
- ☐ No warning lights appearing

---

### 15. PROFESSIONAL INSPECTION
**The ultimate protection:**

☐ **Have a mechanic inspect it before you buy**

**Value:** Potentially thousands in avoided repairs

Any seller who refuses a pre-purchase inspection is hiding something.

## SCORING YOUR INSPECTION

| Score | Recommendation |
|-------|----------------|
| 0-1 red flags | Good candidate — negotiate minor fixes |
| 2-3 red flags | Proceed with caution — get professional inspection |
| 4+ red flags | Walk away — better cars exist |

## NEGOTIATION AMMUNITION

Use this checklist to negotiate price:

> "I found 3 issues that'll cost about $800 to fix. Can you come down $500 on the price?"

Common repair costs:
- Tires (set of 4): $400-800
- Brake job (all 4 wheels): $300-600
- A/C recharge/repair: $200-1,200
- Alignment: $75-150
- Check engine light diagnosis: $50-150
