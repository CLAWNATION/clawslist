- C: "The Mechanic's Secret Checklist"

### Test 2: CTA Button
- A: "Get My Free Checklist"
- B: "Send Me the Checklist"
- C: "Download Now"
- D: "Get Instant Access"

### Test 3: Form Fields
- A: Email only (higher volume)
- B: Email + Name (more personalized follow-up)
- C: Email + Zip Code (local targeting)

### Test 4: Trust Signals
- A: None (control)
- B: "500+ downloads"
- C: "4.9/5 on Google"
- D: Both B + C

### Test 5: Exit Intent Close Button
- A: "No thanks" (neutral)
- B: "No thanks, I like surprise repair bills" (guilt)
- C: "I'll buy without it" (reverse psychology)

## COPY GUIDELINES

### Do:
- ✅ Lead with value, not features
- ✅ Use "you" and "your" (second person)
- ✅ Include specific numbers (15 points, $1,000s saved)
- ✅ Add social proof (downloads, reviews, testimonials)
- ✅ Keep it scannable (bullets, short paragraphs)
- ✅ Match the "honest mechanic" tone

### Don't:
- ❌ Use jargon or technical terms
- ❌ Make it sound too good to be true
- ❌ Hide the unsubscribe option
- ❌ Ask for too much info upfront
- ❌ Use aggressive sales language
