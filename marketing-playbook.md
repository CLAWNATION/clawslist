# Auto Repair Marketing Playbook

## 1. Content Formats

### Primary (High Trust-Building)
| Format | Purpose | Frequency |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| **Before/After Reveals** | Show transformation, build credibility | 2x/week |
| **Mechanic POV Shorts** | Behind-the-scenes, humanize the shop | 3x/week |
| **Myth-Busting Carousels** | Educate, position as expert | 1x/week |
| **Customer Testimonial Videos** | Social proof, real stories | 1x/week |
| **"What This Sound Means"** | Problem-aware content, high searchability | 2x/week |

### Secondary (Community & Engagement)
| Format | Purpose | Frequency |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| **Local Spotlights** | Partner with nearby businesses | 1x/week |
| **"Ask a Mechanic" Lives** | Real-time Q&A, build authority | Bi-weekly |
| **Seasonal Prep Checklists** | Practical value, shareable | Monthly |

## 2. Hook Templates (Copy-Paste Ready)

### The Fear Hook
> "I just saved a customer $2,400 by NOT doing what the dealership recommended..."

### The Curiosity Hook
> "Your check engine light came on. Do you actually need to panic? 90% of drivers get this wrong."

### The Relatability Hook
> "POV: You're a single mom who knows nothing about cars and every mechanic can smell it."

### The Authority Hook
> "I've been fixing [make/model] for 15 years. Here's what actually breaks vs. what dealers claim breaks."

### The Local Hook
> "If you live in [City] and drive a [Popular Local Car], you need to see this."

### The Urgency Hook
> "Winter is coming. If you haven't checked these 3 things, you're gambling with your safety."

## 4. Reply Rules (Engagement Protocol)

### Response Time Targets
- **DMs**: Within 2 hours (business hours)
- **Comments**: Within 4 hours
- **Reviews**: Within 24 hours

### Comment Response Templates

**Question about pricing:**
> "Great question! It depends on [factor]. DM us your car's year/make/model and we'll give you a ballpark — no commitment needed."

**Skeptical comment:**
> "Totally get the skepticism — this industry has earned it. Happy to show you exactly what we found and why. Transparency is our thing."

**Local tag/mention:**
> "Thanks for the love, [Name]! 🙌 See you at the shop?"

**Negative review:**
> "[Name], I'm [Owner Name], the owner. This isn't the experience we want anyone to have. Can we make this right? DM me directly."

### The Golden Rule
Every reply should either:
1. Answer the question directly
2. Move the conversation to DM for details
3. Invite them to the shop

Never argue. Never be defensive. Always be helpful.

## 5. Tone of Voice

### Core Characteristics
- **Direct** — No fluff, no jargon without explanation
- **Honest** — Admit when something is expensive, when you don't know, when a customer got ripped off elsewhere
- **Local** — Use local references, community pride, "we're your neighbors"
- **Confident but not arrogant** — Expert without talking down

### Voice Examples

❌ **Wrong:** "We provide comprehensive automotive solutions leveraging decades of industry expertise."
✅ **Right:** "We've been fixing cars in [City] for 15 years. Here's what we've learned..."

❌ **Wrong:** "Our certified technicians utilize state-of-the-art diagnostic equipment."
✅ **Right:** "We don't guess. We test. Then we show you exactly what we found."

❌ **Wrong:** "Contact us for a consultation regarding your vehicle's needs."
✅ **Right:** "Got a weird noise? Just come by. We'll take a look, no charge."

### Words to Use
- Straightforward
- Fair
- Honest
- Local
- Neighbors
- Family
- Trusted
- No BS

### Words to Avoid
- Comprehensive
- Solutions
- Utilize
- Leverage
- Synergy
- Optimize
- State-of-the-art

## 7. Success Metrics (Track Weekly)

### Vanity Metrics (Awareness)
- Follower growth
- Post reach
- Video views

### Business Metrics (Revenue)
- Leads from social (ask "how'd you hear about us?")
- Email sign-ups
- Appointment bookings attributed to social
- Cost per lead (if running ads)

### Trust Metrics (Long-term)
- Review velocity (new reviews per month)
- Response rate to comments/DMs
- Repeat engagement (people coming back)
