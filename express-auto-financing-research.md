## 1. SBA LOANS (BEST OPTION FOR AUTO REPAIR)

### SBA 7(a) Loan Program

**What it is:** The SBA's primary loan program for small businesses

**Best for:**
- Buying equipment (lifts, diagnostic tools)
- Working capital
- Refinancing existing debt
- Hiring and training

**Loan amounts:** Up to $5 million

**Interest rates:**
- Variable: Prime + 2.25% to 4.75%
- Currently: ~8.5% to 11%

**Terms:**
- Equipment: 10 years
- Working capital: 7 years
- Real estate: 25 years

**Qualifications you'll likely meet:**
- ✅ For-profit business
- ✅ Located in US (San Bruno)
- ✅ Owner equity invested (time/money)
- ✅ Credit score 680+ (recommended)
- ✅ Business plan (we have marketing docs)
- ✅ 2+ years in business (preferred, not required)

**How hiring a veteran helps:**
- SBA has veteran preference programs
- Faster processing for veteran-owned/hiring businesses
- Some lenders offer rate discounts

**Application:**
1. Find SBA-approved lender (see list below)
2. Prepare documents (we have most from business plan)
3. Submit application
4. Approval: 30-60 days

### SBA 504 Loan Program

**What it is:** For major fixed assets (real estate, large equipment)

**Best for:**
- Buying your shop building
- Major equipment ($150K+)
- Renovations/improvements

**Loan structure:**
- 50% bank loan
- 40% CDC (Certified Development Company)
- 10% your down payment

**Amount:** Up to $20 million

**Interest rate:** ~3% fixed on CDC portion

**Qualifications:**
- Net worth under $15 million
- Average net income under $5 million

**Application:**
1. Contact SBA District Office (San Francisco)
2. Work with CDC
3. Find participating lender

## 2. LINES OF CREDIT

### Traditional Business Line of Credit

**Best for:** Managing cash flow, covering payroll, unexpected expenses

**Amounts:** $10,000 to $250,000

**Interest rates:** 7% to 25% (depending on creditworthiness)

**Terms:**
- Revolving (use, repay, reuse)
- Draw period: 1-5 years
- Interest-only payments on what you use

**Where to apply:**
- Your current bank (relationship matters)
- Online lenders (Kabbage, OnDeck, Fundbox)
- Credit unions

**What you'll need:**
- 2+ years in business (ideal)
- $50K+ annual revenue
- 600+ credit score
- Business bank account

**Best for auto repair:**
- Covering parts purchases before customer payment
- Payroll during slow weeks
- Emergency repairs/equipment

### SBA CAPLines Program

**What it is:** SBA-backed line of credit

**Types:**
- **Seasonal CAPLine:** For seasonal cash flow
- **Contract CAPLine:** For specific contracts
- **Builder's CAPLine:** For construction/renovation
- **Working Capital CAPLine:** General use

**Amount:** Up to $5 million

**Interest rate:** Prime + 2.25% to 4.75%

**Advantage:** Lower rates than traditional LOC, but longer approval process

## 3. VETERAN-HIRING PROGRAMS & INCENTIVES

### Work Opportunity Tax Credit (WOTC)

**What it is:** Federal tax credit for hiring veterans

**Amount:**
- Short-term unemployed veteran: $2,400
- Long-term unemployed veteran: $9,600
- Disabled veteran: Up to $9,600

**Qualifications:**
- Veteran unemployed for at least 4 weeks (short-term) or 6 months (long-term)
- Work at least 120 hours in first year

**How to claim:**
1. Apply for certification (Form 8850) within 28 days of hire
2. Submit to state workforce agency
3. Claim credit on tax return

**For Express Auto:** If your Navy veteran mechanic was unemployed for 6+ months, you could get $9,600 tax credit.

### Veterans Employment Pay Program (San Mateo County)

**What it is:** Local program to incentivize veteran hiring

**Potential benefits:**
- Wage subsidies (partial reimbursement for veteran wages)
- Training grants
- Tax incentives

**Contact:**
- San Mateo County Veterans Services
- Phone: (650) 372-6200
- Address: 2000 Alameda de las Pulgas, San Mateo

### California Employment Development Department (EDD)

**Veteran hiring programs:**
- Priority services for veterans
- Tax credits
- Training reimbursements

**Contact:** (866) 490-2049

### SBA Veterans Advantage

**What it is:** SBA loan fee waiver for veteran-owned/hiring businesses

**Benefit:** Waives guarantee fees (0.5-3.5% of loan amount)

**On a $100K loan:** Save $500-$3,500

**Qualifications:**
- Business owned by veteran, OR
- Business commits to hiring veterans

**For Express Auto:** Mention veteran hiring in your application

## 10. RED FLAGS TO AVOID

**Don't Do This:**
- ❌ Apply for multiple loans at once (hurts credit)
- ❌ Take first offer without comparison
- ❌ Ignore the fine print (prepayment penalties)
- ❌ Borrow more than you need
- ❌ Use short-term loans for long-term needs

**Do This Instead:**
- ✅ Shop around (compare 2-3 lenders)
- ✅ Read all documents carefully
- ✅ Have a lawyer/accountant review
- ✅ Understand total cost (interest + fees)
- ✅ Have a clear plan for the money
