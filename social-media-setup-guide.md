# Social Media Setup Guide — Express Auto Repair

## PLATFORMS TO CREATE (Priority Order)

### 1. Google Business Profile (CRITICAL — Do First)
**Why:** 70% of auto repair discovery happens here.

**Setup Checklist:**
- [ ] Go to business.google.com
- [ ] Claim/verify business (postcard verification takes 5-7 days)
- [ ] Add complete info: hours, phone, website, services
- [ ] Upload 10+ photos: exterior, interior, team, work in progress
- [ ] Write description using keywords: "auto repair [city]", "brake repair", "oil change"
- [ ] Add service menu with prices
- [ ] Enable messaging
- [ ] Set up Q&A section (seed with common questions)

**Bio/Description Template:**
```
Express Auto Repair — honest, reliable auto care in [City]. 

✅ ASE certified mechanics
✅ Free estimates & diagnostics
✅ Same-day service on most repairs
✅ 5-star rated on Google

Services: Brakes, oil changes, diagnostics, A/C repair, transmission service, tire rotation.

📞 [Phone]
🌐 expressauto.io
📍 [Address]

"We treat your car like it's our own."
```

### 3. Facebook Business Page
**Why:** Older demographic, community groups, reviews.

**Setup Checklist:**
- [ ] Create at facebook.com/pages/create
- [ ] Add all business details
- [ ] Upload cover photo + profile picture
- [ ] Enable reviews
- [ ] Add "Book Now" button (link to Calendly)
- [ ] Join local community groups (search "[City] residents", "[City] buy sell trade")

**Bio Template:**

```
We believe in:
✓ Transparent pricing
✓ Quality workmanship
✓ Treating customers like neighbors

Free estimates. Same-day service. ASE certified mechanics.

📞 [Phone]
🌐 expressauto.io
```

### 4. TikTok (Optional But Powerful)

## POSTING SCHEDULE (Starter)

### Week 1 (Launch Week)
| Day | Platform | Content |
|-----|----------|---------|
| Mon | All | "We're here!" launch post |
| Tue | IG/FB | Meet the team |
| Wed | IG Reels | Shop tour (15 sec) |
| Thu | FB | Service spotlight: brakes |
| Fri | All | "Weekend ready" customer car |

### Ongoing (Week 2+)
- **Instagram:** 4-5 posts/week (mix of feed + reels)
- **Facebook:** 3-4 posts/week
- **TikTok:** 5-7 short videos/week
- **Google:** 1-2 photos/week, respond to all reviews

## RESPONSE TEMPLATES

### To Positive Reviews
> "Thanks [Name]! 🙌 We're glad we could get you back on the road quickly. See you next time!"

### To Questions
> "Great question! It depends on [factor]. DM us your car's year/make/model and we'll give you a ballpark — no commitment needed."

### To Negative Comments
> "[Name], I'm [Owner Name]. This isn't the experience we want anyone to have. Can we make this right? DM me directly."
