# Web Developer Brief — Express Auto Repair

**Project:** Website Redesign & Development  
**Client:** Express Auto Repair (expressauto.io)  
**Budget:** $2,500-5,000  
**Timeline:** 4-6 weeks  
**Status:** Open for proposals

---

## PROJECT OVERVIEW

Express Auto Repair is a locally-owned auto repair shop seeking a modern, conversion-focused website redesign. The current site is basic and lacks trust signals, lead capture, and mobile optimization.

**Current Site:** expressauto.io  
**Current Issues:**
- No trust bar or social proof
- Missing services grid
- No "How It Works" section
- No lead capture mechanism
- Not mobile-optimized
- Poor conversion path

**Goal:** Create a website that builds trust, captures leads, and drives appointment bookings.

---

## DELIVERABLES

### 1. Website Pages (5 pages)

**Homepage**
- Hero section with trust-focused headline
- Trust bar (ratings, certifications, guarantees)
- Services grid (6-8 services)
- "How It Works" section (4-step process)
- Social proof (testimonials)
- Lead magnet capture (Used Car Checklist)
- FAQ section (accordion)
- Final CTA section

**About Page**
- Company story
- Team section (photos + bios)
- Values/mission
- Credentials/awards
- Trust signals

**Services Page**
- Detailed service descriptions (6-8 services)
- Pricing transparency section
- Warranty information
- CTA for each service

**Contact Page**
- Contact form
- Map integration
- Hours/phone/email
- Emergency note
- Click-to-call (mobile)

**Blog** (template only, content provided)
- Blog listing page
- Individual blog post template
- Category/tag support

### 2. Technical Features

**Lead Capture System:**
- Email signup forms (modal, inline, footer)
- Mailchimp integration (API key provided)
- Welcome email automation

**Google Business Profile Integration:**
- Reviews widget (embed live reviews)
- Map embed
- Click for directions

**Performance:**
- Page speed: <3 seconds (Lighthouse 90+)
- Mobile-responsive (all breakpoints)
- SEO-optimized (meta tags, schema markup)
- SSL certificate

**Analytics:**
- Google Analytics 4 setup
- Google Tag Manager
- Conversion tracking (form submissions, calls)
- Heatmap tracking (Hotjar or similar)

### 3. Content Management

**CMS:** WordPress preferred (or Webflow/Framer if developer recommends)

## DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

### Brand Guidelines

**Colors:**
- Primary: #1e3a5f (Navy blue)
- Secondary: #2d5a87 (Medium blue)
- Accent: #ff6b35 (Orange)
- Success: #4ade80 (Green)

- Background: #f8fafc (Light gray)

**Typography:**
- Headings: Inter (700-800 weight)

- Sizes: 42px (hero), 28px (H2), 18px (H3), 16px (body)
