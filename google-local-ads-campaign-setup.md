## PHASE 6: TRACKING & MEASUREMENT

### Set Up Conversion Tracking

**1. Phone Call Tracking:**
- Use Google forwarding number (free)
- Track calls from ads
- Record call duration
- Mark qualified leads

**2. Form Submission Tracking:**
- Add Google Ads conversion tag to thank-you page
- Track "Contact Us" form submissions
- Track "Get Quote" form submissions

**3. Direction Request Tracking:**
- Track "Get Directions" clicks
- Import from Google Business Profile

**4. Booking Tracking:**
- If using online booking, track as conversion
- Value: $200-500 (average job value)

### Key Metrics to Watch

| Metric | Target | How to Improve |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Click-Through Rate (CTR) | 5%+ | Better ad copy, extensions |
| Cost Per Click (CPC) | $3-8 | Quality score, relevance |
| Cost Per Lead | $15-35 | Better targeting, landing pages |
| Conversion Rate | 15%+ | Landing page optimization |
| Quality Score | 7+ | Keyword relevance, landing page |

## COMPETITOR RESEARCH

### What to Check

1. **Search "auto repair [city]"**
   - Who's advertising?
   - What are they saying?
   - What's their offer?

2. **Use Google Ads Keyword Planner**
   - Search volume for your keywords
   - Estimated CPC
   - Competition level

3. **SpyFu or SEMrush (optional)**
   - See competitor keywords
   - Estimate their budget
   - View their ad copy

### Differentiation Strategy

**If competitors emphasize:**
- **Price:** Emphasize trust/quality
- **Speed:** Emphasize thoroughness/warranty
- **24/7:** Emphasize expertise/certifications

**Your angle:** "Honest mechanics, transparent pricing, neighborly service"

---

## TROUBLESHOOTING

### High CPC ($10+)

**Causes:**
- Low Quality Score
- Too broad keywords
- High competition
- Poor ad relevance

**Fixes:**
- Add more specific keywords
- Improve ad copy relevance
- Optimize landing pages
- Use exact match keywords

### Low CTR (< 2%)

**Causes:**
- Weak ad copy
- Wrong keywords
- Poor ad extensions

**Fixes:**
- Add numbers ($35 oil changes)
- Include urgency ("Same-day service")
- Use all ad extensions
- Test new headlines

### Low Conversion (< 10%)

**Causes:**
- Poor landing page
- Slow load time
- No clear CTA
- Mismatch with ad

**Fixes:**
- Create dedicated landing pages
- Speed up page load (<3 seconds)
- Add click-to-call button
- Match landing page to ad copy
